About MediStock

MediStock is a college project developed to help users check the availability of medicines at nearby medical stores. Instead of visiting different stores to find a medicine, users can check the available stock through the system.

How It Works

1. Select Medicine

↓

↓

3. View Available Stock

↓

4. Visit the Store

Benefits

Saves time
Easy to check stock
Shows multiple medical stores
Helps users find medicines faster
